Questions & Answers

Good to
know.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes — members can freeze their membership for medical reasons or travel. Speak to our team at the reception desk and we'll sort it out.

Is my first session really free?

Absolutely. No strings attached. Book your free intro session, come in, train, and decide if Grand Fitness is right for you.

Are group classes really included?

Yes — all group classes (Boxing, CrossFit, Aerobic, Pilates, Armwrestling, Group Exercise) are included in every membership at no extra cost.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Of course. You can upgrade to a longer plan at any time and we'll adjust the price accordingly. Just come to reception.

Do I need to bring my own towel?

We recommend bringing your own towel for training. Locker rooms are modern and well-equipped — lockers are available for all members.

How do I book personal training?

Personal training sessions can be booked directly with any of our coaches or at reception. Your first session is always free for new members.
