What This Class Is

The foundation
everyone skips.

Pilates is systematic training for the muscles you can't see in the mirror: the deep core that stabilizes your spine, the stabilizers around your hips and shoulders, and the postural chain that holds you upright through every workday. In a mat class with Ninutsa, every movement has a purpose, every breath has a place, and 'just going through the motions' is physically impossible — the precision IS the workout.

Don't mistake calm for easy. Pilates is famously humbling for strong people: powerlifters shake on exercises that look gentle, and desk workers discover muscles they didn't know had names. What everyone leaves with is the same — standing taller, moving smoother, and understanding their body in a way no other training provides. It's the class that makes every other class better.

Questions & Answers

Before your
first class.

Isn't Pilates just easy stretching?

Come find out. 😄 Pilates looks gentle and trains brutally honest — controlled movements with constant deep-core engagement humble strong athletes weekly. It's low-impact, yes. Easy, absolutely not. And that combination is exactly why it works.

Is this class only for women?

Not remotely — though the myth persists. Pilates was invented by a man, refined training boxers, and some of our most regular mats belong to lifters and fighters protecting their athletic careers. Core and stability have no gender.

I have back problems. Can I participate?

Pilates is famously back-friendly — many members come specifically because of desk-related back grumbling, and a strong core plus mobile hips is the best daily insurance a spine can have. For diagnosed conditions or injuries: check with your doctor first, then tell Ninutsa — she'll adapt every exercise for you.

Do I need to be flexible to start?

No — that's like needing to be strong before lifting weights. Flexibility and mobility are what the class BUILDS. Wherever your range of motion is today is exactly the right starting point.

What do I wear and bring?

Comfortable, somewhat fitted clothes (so Ninutsa can see your alignment), water, and socks if you like. Mats and all equipment are provided — just bring the body.

How is Pilates different from yoga?

Cousins, not twins: yoga blends movement with breath and mindfulness traditions; Pilates is a systematic strength-and-control method laser-focused on the core and precise alignment. If your goal is core strength, posture and athletic stability — this is the one.
