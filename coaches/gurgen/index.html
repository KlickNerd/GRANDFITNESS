About Gurgen

The story.

Let me be direct: there are a lot of gyms in this city. Most of them are fine. Grand Fitness is different — and I say that not because I have to, but because I designed every square meter of it myself, hired every coach personally, and trained every single one of them to meet my standards.

My standards are high. That's not arrogance — that's the reason our members get results. I've been coaching for over a decade, trained beginners who became athletes, athletes who became champions, and people who just wanted to feel good in their own skin again. All of them had one thing in common: they needed someone who actually knew what they were doing.

That's what you get at Grand Fitness. Not just equipment. Not just classes. A team of people who give a damn — led by me.
