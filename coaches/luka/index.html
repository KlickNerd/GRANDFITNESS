About Luka

The approach.

Luka comes from the hard end of the strength world: eight years in armwrestling, powerlifting and various strength sports, backed by more than five years of active fitness coaching and extensive hands-on experience in strength training, physical conditioning and body transformation.

His main principle is an individualized approach — every program is tailored to the person's goals, physical abilities and level of experience, with particular emphasis on proper and safe exercise execution, appropriate load selection and gradual progression.

Whether your goal is gaining muscle, losing weight, or finally learning proper strength-training technique, Luka structures your workouts so progress is measurable — and keeps coming.
