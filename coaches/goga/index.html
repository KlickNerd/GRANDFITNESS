About Goga

The story.

Goga spent years in competitive boxing before turning to coaching — and it shows. He's one of the most technically gifted coaches in the gym: precision, discipline, and a whole lot of heart.

His classes at Grand Fitness are built around movement, combinations, and bag work. No sparring, no getting hit — just pure technique, power, and full-body conditioning that will push you harder than any cardio machine ever could.

Whether you want to learn real boxing fundamentals or just want the best conditioning workout in Batumi, Goga's corner is where you'll find it.
