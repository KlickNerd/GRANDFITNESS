About Otar

The story.

Otar found CrossFit the way most people do — by getting absolutely humbled by his first WOD. He's been chasing that challenge ever since, first as a competitive athlete, then as a coach.

His sessions are constantly varied, relentlessly challenging, and somehow always fun — even when your lungs are on fire. Behind the intensity is careful programming: every workout is scaled to the person in front of him, from first-timers to seasoned athletes.

For Otar, CrossFit isn't about being the fittest person in the room. It's about being fitter than you were last month — measurably, undeniably, every single time.
