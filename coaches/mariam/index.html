About Mariam

The story.

Mariam discovered her love for fitness not in a competition, but in the quiet transformation she witnessed in her clients — the growing confidence, the stronger posture, the brighter energy.

With a background in sports science and years of hands-on coaching experience, Mariam combines evidence-based training methodology with genuine personal attention. She meets every client exactly where they are.

At Grand Fitness, Mariam specializes in helping women build strength and confidence in an environment that feels supportive, not intimidating.
