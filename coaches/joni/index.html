About Joni

The story.

Pure energy, every single session — that's Joni. His classes have a waiting list for a reason: he makes cardio genuinely something to look forward to, which might be the rarest coaching skill of all.

Joni leads both Aerobic and Group Exercise at Grand Fitness. His aerobic sessions are classic cardio done right — great music, great energy, constant movement. His group exercise classes mix strength, cardio and functional work so no two sessions are ever the same.

Behind the fun is a serious coach: Joni watches form, scales intensity for every level in the room, and makes absolutely sure that beginners feel just as at home as regulars.
