About Rezo

The story.

Rezo is Batumi's only dedicated armwrestling coach — and one of the few in the region who brings genuine competitive-level expertise to a discipline most gyms don't even offer.

Armwrestling is a real sport: it demands grip strength, technique, leverage, and explosive power, all trained systematically. Rezo's sessions cover table technique, specialized grip and forearm work, and the strategic side of the sport that separates winners from strong arms.

Whether you want to compete or just want the strongest handshake in the room, Rezo will get you there — and you'll have a great time doing it.
