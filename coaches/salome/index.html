About Salome

The story.

Salome came to coaching the way many of her members come to the gym: unsure at first, then completely hooked. She knows exactly how intimidating a training floor can feel on day one — and she's built her entire coaching style around removing that feeling.

Her sessions at Grand Fitness are training built around women, for women: real strength work, honest effort, and a group atmosphere where nobody is judged and everybody is pushed. Whether it's a first workout ever or a return after a long break, Salome meets every woman exactly where she is.

The results speak Georgian, Russian and English alike: stronger bodies, better posture, and — her favorite metric — women who walk into the gym like they own the place. Because at Grand Fitness, they do.
