About Nika

The story.

If you want to get strong — really strong — Nika is your person. Methodical, data-driven, and deeply knowledgeable about the science of strength training, he approaches every client like an engineering problem with a human at the center.

His programs are built on the fundamentals: squat, bench, deadlift, progressive overload, and recovery that's tracked as carefully as the training itself. No fads, no guesswork — just numbers that go up, month after month.

Nika works with complete beginners learning their first barbell lifts and with experienced lifters chasing personal records. The method is the same; only the loads change.
