About Nino

The approach.

Nino — known to everyone in the gym as Ninutsa — is a Fitness School graduate and certified personal and group fitness trainer, skilled in proper exercise technique, effective use of fitness equipment and building individualized programs around each client's needs.

She works with clients both one-on-one and in group settings, with a personalized, results-oriented approach. For Nino, training is not only physical transformation — it matters just as much that clients enjoy the process, recognize their own progress and stay motivated at every stage.

Her sessions are built on proper technique, quality execution and continuous progress monitoring — so your training stays safe, effective and consistent from week to week.
