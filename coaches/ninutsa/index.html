About Ninutsa

The story.

Ninutsa believes the foundation every athlete overlooks is the one that matters most. Her Pilates classes build the deep core strength, posture, and body control that make every other discipline easier — from boxing to powerlifting.

Her coaching style is precise and calm: every movement has a purpose, every breath has a place. Members leave her classes standing taller, moving better, and understanding their bodies in a way no other training provides.

At Grand Fitness, Ninutsa also leads mobility and recovery work — the quiet, unglamorous training that keeps the loud, heavy training possible year after year.
