method:
  eyebrow: "The Grand Fitness Boxing Method"
  title: "Four phases.<br>From zero to boxer."
  lead: "Boxing is a skill, and skills have an order. Goga teaches it the way he learned it — properly, from the ground up:"
  phases:
    - when: "Weeks 1–2"
      title: "Stance, Guard & the Jab"
      text: "Everything in boxing is built on your base. You'll learn the stance that keeps you balanced, the guard that keeps you protected, and how to move without crossing your feet. Then comes the most important punch in boxing: the jab — plus the cross behind it. Shadowboxing in front of the mirror might feel funny on day one; by day five it starts feeling like boxing."
    - when: "Weeks 3–8"
      title: "Combinations & the Bag"
      text: "Now your hands learn to talk to each other: hooks and uppercuts join the arsenal, and single punches become combinations — the 1-2, the 1-2-3, and slips and rolls woven in between. You'll spend real rounds on the heavy bag learning to sit down on your punches, breathe with your rhythm, and keep technique clean when your arms get heavy. This is where the addiction usually starts."
    - when: "Month 3+"
      title: "Pads, Flow & Fight-Fitness"
      text: "Pad work with your coach: combinations called and answered in real time, counters, rhythm changes, defense flowing into offense. Your footwork starts happening without thinking, and the rounds-based conditioning quietly turns you into one of the fittest people you know. Every session still ends with your heart pounding and your head completely clear."
    - when: "Ongoing"
      title: "Sharpen & Choose Your Path"
      text: "From here, boxing is yours. Most members keep sharpening technique and chasing that unbeatable post-session feeling. Some add strength work for more power. And if — only if — you ever want to test yourself further, Goga will tell you honestly when you're ready for the next step. There is never any pressure: this room is about skill, not bravado."

faq:
  eyebrow: "Questions & Answers"
  title: "Before you<br>ask."
  items:
    - q: "Will I get punched?"
      a: "No. Our boxing classes are built entirely on technique, bags and pads — nobody spars, nobody gets hit, ever. If you one day decide you want controlled technical sparring, that's a conversation you start with Goga — never the other way around."
    - q: "I'm completely unfit. Can I still start?"
      a: "Yes — boxing is how you'll GET fit, and it's one of the most enjoyable ways ever invented. Every drill and every round is scaled to your level; in your first weeks, you rest as much as you need. Within two months your engine will surprise you."
    - q: "Is boxing suitable for women?"
      a: "Absolutely — some of the most technical boxers in our classes are women. Boxing rewards speed, rhythm and precision far more than raw size, and everyone trains together in the same supportive room."
    - q: "What equipment do I need?"
      a: "For your first sessions: just training clothes and water — we'll sort you out with gear so you can simply try it. Once you're hooked (you will be), your own wraps and gloves are the first investment, and Goga will happily tell you exactly what to buy."
    - q: "How long until I can actually box?"
      a: "You'll throw your first clean 1-2 in week one. After eight weeks of two sessions per week, you'll shadowbox real combinations, work the bag with proper technique, and feel like a different athlete. Boxing is a lifelong skill — but it rewards you from day one."
    - q: "Am I too old to learn boxing?"
      a: "If you can walk briskly, you can learn to box. We have members in their forties and fifties throwing crisp combinations — the intensity adapts to you, and the coordination work is one of the best things you can do for your brain at any age."
