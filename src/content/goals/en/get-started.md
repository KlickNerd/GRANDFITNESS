  lead: "In twelve weeks, 'I'm thinking about joining a gym' becomes 'I'm a person who trains.' Here's the exact road:"
  phases:
    - when: "Day 1"
      title: "Your First Session"
      text: "You walk in — no appointment needed, any time between 08:00 and 24:00. At reception, we pair you with the coach who fits you best. You talk: your goals, your worries, your starting point. Then a tour, an easy guided first workout that meets you exactly where you are, and the sauna to finish.     - when: "Weeks 1–4"
      title: "Learn the Basics"
      text: "Your coach builds you a simple full-body plan — a handful of fundamental movements, mostly on beginner-friendly machines at first, moving toward free weights as your confidence grows. Two to three visits a week, about an hour each. The only real goal of this phase: make showing up feel normal. Muscles will ache a little, questions will get answered, and the machines will stop looking like alien technology remarkably fast."
    - when: "Weeks 5–12"
      title: "Find Your Thing"
      text: "With the basics down, you get to explore: try a class (Aerobic is famously beginner-friendly), test the cardio zone at the window, maybe throw a first jab in the boxing room. Somewhere in these weeks, something clicks — a class you love, a lift you're proud of, a goal that excites you. When it does, your coach shapes your plan around it. This is where 'exercising' quietly becomes 'training'."
    - when: "Month 3+"
      title: "You're a Gym Person Now"
      text: "The routine is set, the first visible results have arrived, and the gym has become a normal — maybe favorite — part of your week. From here, every door in Grand Fitness is open: pick a bigger goal from our Goals programs, chase strength with Gurgen, join Salome's women's sessions, or simply keep enjoying your routine. Look back at week one. That person wouldn't believe where you are."
