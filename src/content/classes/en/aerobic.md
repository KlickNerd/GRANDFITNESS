Aerobic is continuous rhythmic movement set to music — step patterns, low-impact combinations and full-body sequences that keep your heart rate in the endurance zone for the whole class. No complicated choreography to memorize, no dance background needed: Joni builds every sequence up step by step, layer by layer, so your body learns it while you're already moving.

The physiology is beautifully simple: sustained aerobic work strengthens your heart, multiplies your endurance, teaches your body to burn fat efficiently, and floods your brain with the endorphins that made 'runner's high' famous — minus the running. The music does the pacing, the group does the motivating, and 45 minutes disappear faster than any treadmill session you've ever survived.
