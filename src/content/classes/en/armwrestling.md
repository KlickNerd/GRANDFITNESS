Everyone has armwrestled; almost nobody has learned to. That gap is exactly what this class fills. Armwrestling is a deeply technical sport — hand position, wrist control, body leverage and timing decide matches long before raw strength does, which is why trained 75-kilo athletes routinely beat untrained giants. Rezo teaches that technical arsenal from zero: hooks, toprolls, presses, defense, and the strategy of reading an opponent at the table.

Around the table work sits the sport's legendary strength training: hands, wrists, forearms and connective tissue built with specialized equipment most gyms have never seen. The side effects are extremely practical — a crushing grip transfers to every deadlift, every pull-up and every stubborn jar lid, and forearms that fill sleeves are simply part of the package. Whether you want to compete one day or just want to be dangerous at the table, this is where it's built.
