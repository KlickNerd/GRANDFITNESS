Group Exercise is the Swiss army knife of our schedule: one session that trains strength, cardio and coordination together. One day it's a circuit of dumbbells, kettlebells and bodyweight stations; the next it's intervals, sleds and battle ropes; the day after, a partner workout that has the whole room laughing between sets. The only constant is the format — coached, scalable, and over before you can get bored.

The secret ingredient is the room itself. Training alone, your brain negotiates ('maybe eight reps is enough today'). Training in a group, with music up and a coach counting you down, those negotiations simply never start. It's the most reliable effort-multiplier in fitness — and the reason group training members are famously the most consistent people in any gym.
