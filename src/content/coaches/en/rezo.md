Rezo is a university-trained coach in the truest sense: a graduate of the Coaching Faculty at the State University of Physical Education and Sport, a graduate of Irakli Kenchoshvili's renowned Fitness School, and a certified physical education and sports teacher.

His philosophy is built on one conviction — the best results are achieved through an individualized approach. Every program Rezo writes is tailored to the client's goals, physical abilities and health condition, with particular emphasis on proper exercise technique, safe training loads and gradual progression.

Each personal session lasts around 60 minutes and comes with the guidance that makes results stick: practical recommendations on nutrition and a healthy lifestyle, adjusted as you progress.
