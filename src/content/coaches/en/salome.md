Salome has worked in the fitness industry for almost eight years, both as a personal trainer and as a group fitness instructor — and she leads the Women's Fitness program at Grand Fitness.

Her core principle is an individualized, attentive approach: she closely monitors not just exercise performance, but each client's physical condition, abilities, response to training loads and overall progress. Programs and workloads are tailored to individual goals and fitness levels, evaluated regularly, and adjusted whenever necessary — so progress stays safe, effective and consistent.

She specializes in weight loss, muscle gain, body shaping and toning, works with complete beginners and experienced trainees alike, and creates individualized nutrition plans around each client's specific goals. And she does all of it in four languages: Georgian, Russian, Turkish and English.
