I'm Nika, 25 years old, and sport has been the center of my life for the past ten years. The first five, I built my body the functional way — running, push-ups, pull-ups, endurance work. No machines, no shortcuts: just me against gravity. The last five years, I've trained seriously in the gym — and the numbers on this page are what that decade built.

For three years now, I've been coaching as a personal trainer: helping people improve their body composition, get stronger, build muscle, and reach goals they set for themselves. Having walked the whole road myself — from bodyweight basics to a 240kg deadlift — I understand exactly where you are, wherever you're starting from.
