Joni has been active in sport since 2011 — a motivated, goal-oriented fitness instructor with hands-on experience in personal training and body transformation.

He helps people reach their goals through weight loss, muscle development and building a genuinely healthy lifestyle, creating individualized workout and nutrition plans based on each client's goals, physical condition and capabilities.

In the studio, you'll meet him at the front of the Aerobic and Group Exercise classes — the same energy, discipline and attention to technique, shared with the whole room.
