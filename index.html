About Grand Fitness

More than
a gym.

Grand Fitness was built on one belief: every person deserves a place to become a better version of themselves. Not just with good equipment — but with the right people around them, the right guidance, and an environment that makes showing up feel worth it.

We're Batumi's newest and most ambitious fitness club. Our team of 9 certified coaches works with athletes, beginners, and everyone in between — because progress looks different for everyone, and that's exactly the point.
